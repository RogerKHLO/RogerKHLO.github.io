### The Brief
Design and prototype an automated **Vertical Reciprocating Conveyor (VRC)** to move up to 3 kg of material across a 1500 mm vertical range at a minimum of 300 mm/s, with safeguards against overloading and overtravel.

### The Build
- **Lifting:** 2:1 underslung cable-pulley hoist.
- **Drive:** 24 V Maxon RE 65 brushed DC motor with a custom two-stage 19:1 spur gearbox.
- **Motor control:** ESCON 70/10 controller.
- **Payload validation:** TAL220 load cell.
- **Object detection:** E18-D80NK infrared sensor.
- **Travel limits:** TZ-7141 mechanical limit switches.

### The Result
A full SOLIDWORKS design plus a scaled-down proof-of-concept prototype.
