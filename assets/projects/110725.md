### The Objective
Introduce Mechatronics to prospective students in one fun afternoon.

### The Execution
A 3D-printed chassis on an **Arduino Uno R3**, driven from Python via **pyFirmata**, with **CVZone** face tracking, **VOSK** offline speech and the **Gemini API** for conversation.

### The Outcome
Students left with working Python-controlled robots and first exposure to computer vision and LLM integration.
