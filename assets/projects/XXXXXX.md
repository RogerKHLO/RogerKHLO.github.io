### The Objective
Automate quality control on large surfaces, where manual inspection is slow and inconsistent. The system combines **YOLO** detection architectures with **path-planning** methods so a mobile robot can cover a surface and flag defects.

### The Toolkit
**SLAM** and **ROS** for mapping and navigation, **SIFT** and **LoFTR** for feature extraction and matching, and image processing for the detection pipeline.

### The Status
Ongoing research. Findings so far are presented as a poster.
