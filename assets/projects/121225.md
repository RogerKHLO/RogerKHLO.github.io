### The Brief
Engineer the mechanical skeleton of a smart tool storage machine that retrieves and returns 5kg baskets unattended, without back-driving the load on power loss.

### The Execution
**Z-axis:** worm-gear motor (self-locking, 49.05N hold). **X-axis:** planetary gear motor, 0.28 Nm peak torque for the 8kg gantry. **Y-axis:** NEMA 17 with GT2 belts to overcome 14.7N tray friction.

### The Roadmap
- Suspension drive mount
- Rotary encoder for Z positioning
- Passive alignment grooves
- Soft-robotic gripper

### The Result
Coordinated XYZ motion with no back-driving risk, giving a rigid base for the electronics.
