### The Brief
Vision-guided manipulation exercises on **ABB IRB200** robot arms. A fixed camera, calibrated through field-of-view and **homography** calibration, locates objects so the arm can act on them.

### The Tasks
- **Block stacking:** fixed camera with field-of-view calibration.
- **Peg-in-hole:** inserting a palette block, using field-of-view and homography calibration.

### The Setup
Programming and simulation in **ABB RobotStudio** with the teach pendant, and image processing in **MATLAB**.
