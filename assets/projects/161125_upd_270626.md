### The Idea
A lithophane is a 3D print whose thickness varies with image brightness. Thin areas let more light through and thick areas block it, so the picture only appears when the print is backlit. Two ways I've used it so far.

### Part 1: The Name Card (Nov 2025)
A business card that works differently from paper: held up to a light, it reveals a portrait of me, with my name and a QR code alongside.

![Lithophane name card held up to the light](images/projects/161125.png)

### Part 2: Lightbox Photo Frames (Jun 2026)
Two gifts: wooden-framed lightboxes that read as white relief carvings when off and glow into full photographs when on.

- **Coloured lithophane:** each winter group photo is printed as a relief panel, shown in colour when lit.
- **Frame:** a wooden photo frame holds the panel, with a recessed cavity behind it.
- **Backlight:** a 3-mode 5V LED strip loops around the cavity, powered over USB through an inline switch.
