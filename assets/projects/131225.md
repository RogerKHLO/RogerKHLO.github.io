### The Lineage
This project hardens my earlier capstone **Ward Monitoring System**: FreeRTOS on ESP32 publishing sensor data over MQTT to a MongoDB-backed web dashboard, with LD2410B radar presence detection, DHT11 sensing and a NeoPixel status ring.

### The Brief
Critical infrastructure demands absolute data integrity. The objective was a hardened IoT solution enforcing a "Zero Trust" model: strong encryption, role-based access control and automated defense logic.

### The Execution
**Dual-core architecture:** Core 0 handles the TLS handshake, Core 1 manages the LD2410 radar and DHT11 sensors, linked by FreeRTOS queues. **Mutual TLS** is enforced against a 3-tier PKI (Root, Intermediate, End-Entity).

### The Result
Even a "rogue" device with a valid certificate is rejected by the ACL. Packet capture confirmed total obfuscation of application-layer data.
