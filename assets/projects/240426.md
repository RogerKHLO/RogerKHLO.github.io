### The Brief
Engineer an automated industrial robotic work cell to assemble a **six-part mechanical pen**, replacing a manual manufacturing process.

### Key Contributions
- **Design for Assembly (DFA):** optimised part geometries to ease automated handling.
- **Gripper:** a custom four-zone pneumatic gripper that handles every component in sequence without tool changes.
- **Feeders:** passive vibratory bowl feeders that deliver parts in the correct orientation.
- **Safety:** the work cell is enclosed in a three-zone safety architecture.

### The Result
Cycle time per unit drops from **19.83 s to 10.29 s**, with an estimated **87.42% ROI** and a **1.02-year payback period**.
