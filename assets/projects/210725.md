### The Brief
Replace paper logs and messy chats with a lightweight, mobile-first system that handles fuzzy input like "Table for 4 next Friday".

### The Execution
A secure HTTPS webhook (`doPost`) parses Telegram payloads; a regex date parser handles inputs like "21/7". `/cancel +60123456789 21/7` finds and removes the record, then confirms in chat.

### The Result
A zero-cost central system with no double-bookings and minutes of staff training.
