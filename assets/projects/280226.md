### The Placement
Eleven weeks of hands-on automation work, starting in the drafter's office and moving out to client sites, with SunVision's Sejingkat factory as the base.

### Drafting & Documentation
Prepared and revised **ECAD drawings** in AutoCAD for switchboards and control panels, then produced **as-built drawings** for the SEPOM site reflecting the final layout and commissioning changes.

### Beckhoff HMI & PLC
- Supported commissioning of a Beckhoff HMI soft-PLC setup (CP2212 Panel PC, EK1100 EtherCAT coupler) with **TwinCAT XAE** and **TwinCAT HMI**.
- Worked through remote troubleshooting sessions with Beckhoff support and applied a **downgrade strategy** for compatibility between the HMI project and the legacy IPC runtime.
- Supported the Beckhoff HMI at the Song Water Treatment Plant in Kapit, resolving I/O mismatches and data-scaling issues, and later corrected its I/O mapping and visualisation.

### SEPOM Biogas Plant (Serian)
- Wiring inspection, connectivity testing and cable-routing checks for sensors, pumps, flare tower and MSB I/O, including finding unmarked or mis-pulled cables.
- PLC programming for a remote pump in **Schneider Machine Expert**, and **SCADA-PLC integration** on the Aveva-Schneider platform.
- Network setup: media converters, CPE210 units, remote-PLC communications, Modbus slave IDs, a Waveshare gateway and a Starlink link.
- Documented the network and data topology.

### Field Diagnostics
Modified float switches affected by debris, diagnosed miscalibrated meters, repaired and re-sealed a damaged flowmeter, corrected relay-base wiring, fixed signal drops caused by loose CPE mounts, and helped install separate UPS units for the PC, PLC and field devices. The site was largely complete by week 8, pending a replacement soft starter.
